Shift Slate is an offline volunteer shift planner for small, in-person events. Coordinators create events, configure shift slots with required helper counts, maintain private volunteer contact notes, assign helpers, track coverage gaps and overlaps, and mark attendance locally. No accounts, internet connection, or cloud services are used. Data stays on your device until you manually export a CSV roster through a native file or share dialog. Roster export includes volunteer names and attendance but excludes private contact notes. JSON backup and restore are planned for future updates.
